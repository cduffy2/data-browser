# Tone of voice

How to write anything users see in the prototype: headings, labels, buttons, descriptions, caveats and notices.

Sources: Zeroheight → Brand messaging ([Core Messaging](https://pathways.zeroheight.com/styleguide/s/102143/p/308df1), [Preferred Terms](https://pathways.zeroheight.com/styleguide/s/102143/p/74bff2), [General Guidance](https://pathways.zeroheight.com/styleguide/s/102143/p/4955da)), plus copy on the live site.

## Voice

Pathways is **clear, practical and respectful**. Users are policymakers, donors, analysts and implementers, with a wide range of data literacy.

- **Plain language first.** Explain what a number means, not just what it is. "Woman did not receive any antenatal care visit for her most recent birth", not "ANC0 = 1".
- **Practical, not promotional.** Say what the user can do: "Compare health outcomes across segments". Avoid hype ("transform", "revolutionise").
- **Respectful about people.** Segments describe circumstances, not character. Be careful with "vulnerable": say what people are vulnerable *to* ("vulnerability to poor health"), and never use it as a label for individuals.
- **Woman-centred.** Data is mostly about women and their children; write from their circumstances.

## Mechanics

- **UK English**: organisation, programme, analyse, colour, behaviour.
- **Sentence case** for headings, buttons, tabs, nav and labels ("Change data point", not "Change Data Point"). Never ALL CAPS. Event titles are the exception: capitalise key words.
- **No full stop** after headings or button labels. Use full stops in sub-headings and descriptions that are full sentences.
- **Dates:** 1 October 2025 · Monday, 1 October · Monday, 1 October 2025. Blog cards use the live format "Sep 29, 2026".
- **Times:** 12-hour with time zone: "10–10:30 am BST".
- **Numbers:** percentages as "45%", with no space. Segment codes as "R3.1".

## UI copy patterns

Taken from the live site. Reuse these before writing new ones.

| Element | Pattern | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Page title | Short noun phrase | "Data browser", "Comparison tool", "Pathways resources" |
| Page intro | One or two sentences: what you can do here | "Explore how population segments experience individual health outcomes, behaviours, and vulnerability factors." |
| Primary button | Verb + object | "Join the Pathways Community", "Change data point", "Explore segmentation data" |
| Link CTA | Verb, with an arrow | "Read more →", "View all →", "Back to Blog" |
| Menu items | Verb + object | "Share this view", "Download", "Show standard error" |
| Toggles | Noun + state | "Standard error · off" |
| Tags | Single noun, sentence case | "Article", "Case study", "Community event", "Newsletter" |
| Section titles | Noun phrase | "Highlights", "Latest updates", "Interactive explainers", "Documentation" |
| Closing a sheet | Plain instruction | "Close this window" |

## Data copy

- **Data point names** match live exactly and are reused everywhere (chart title, list, export).
- **Description**: one plain sentence starting with who and what. "The percentage of children under 5 years with fever or cough symptoms who did not receive any medical care or treatment from a healthcare provider."
- **Denominator**: who was asked, with a "Denominator:" label. "Women age 15-49 with a live birth in the last 3 years".
- **Benchmarks**: "Sample average" (bar charts), "Average" (stacked bars), "Sample median" (box plots).
- **Missing data**: "N/A". Never "0" or "No data available".

## Caveats and notices

State the limitation, then what it means for the user. Keep it to one or two sentences.

| Situation | Copy |
|---|---|
| Survey not representative below national level | Explain that results for smaller areas are estimates, and make it dismissible once read. |
| Segment excluded | "One urban segment is not included here (1% of the population) due to uncertainty in estimates owing to its very small size." |
| Tool not optimised for small screens | Title "Limited mobile experience", body "This page works best on larger screens.", actions "Continue to desktop version" / "Back to previous page" |
| Prototype data | Never imply it's real. If a demo might be mistaken for real findings, label it "Illustrative data". |
